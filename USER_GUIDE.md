# FocusGuard - End-User Guide & Handbook

Welcome to **FocusGuard**, your distraction-free digital wellbeing companion. FocusGuard helps you take back control of your attention by intentionally locking away digital distractions while safeguarding your peace of mind.

---

## 1. Quick Start: Your First Focus Session

1. **Launch FocusGuard**: Open the app. The dashboard displays your daily progress ring and quick-action chips.
2. **Choose Duration**: Tap one of the 1-tap quick start chips (**15m**, **25m Pomodoro**, **45m Deep Work**, **60m Study**) or tap **"Start Custom Session"**.
3. **Select a Profile**: Select **Work**, **Study**, **Deep Focus**, or **Sleep**.
4. **Choose Restriction Level**:
   - **Gentle**: Friendly mindfulness reminder dialogs when you open distracting apps.
   - **Moderate**: Full-screen block overlay with an optional pause friction cooldown.
   - **Strict**: Aggressive block overlay requiring a PIN challenge or phrase typing to override.
   - **Locked**: Complete device kiosk lockout. Zero overrides permitted until the timer expires.
5. **Tap "Start Focus"**: The session engages immediately!

---

## 2. Managing Breaks

Need a breather during a long session?
1. On the **Active Session HUD**, tap the amber **"Take Break"** button.
2. The timer switches to an amber ring displaying your remaining break minutes (e.g. 5 minutes).
3. Distracting apps are temporarily unblocked during your break.
4. When the break reaches 00:00, restrictions automatically resume.
5. Each profile has a configurable break quota (e.g. max 2 breaks per session) to prevent procrastination loops.

---

## 3. Override Policies & Intentional Friction

FocusGuard does not make quitting easy, but respects your autonomy through intentional friction:

- **Cooldown Delay**: When you request an override, a countdown (1 to 15 minutes) starts. You must wait out the delay before restrictions lift.
- **Mindful Phrase**: You must accurately type a 100-character mindful passage (e.g. *"I am choosing to pause my focus session intentionally and will return shortly."*).
- **Salted PIN**: Requires entering a secure PIN code you configured earlier (or gave to an accountability partner).
- **Locked Mode**: No overrides allowed. Only the timer expiration will release the phone.

---

## 4. Emergency Access & Safety (Always Unblockable)

Your safety is paramount. The telephone dialer and crisis lines can **never** be blocked:
- Even in **Locked Mode**, tapping **"Emergency Exit"** on the Active Session HUD or launching your phone's dialer opens immediately without friction.
- Dedicated 1-tap crisis calling for **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), **911**, and **112** is accessible from the **Emergency** screen at all times.

---

## 5. Recurring Schedules & Overnight Bedtime

1. Open the **Schedules** tab from the bottom navigation or sidebar.
2. Tap **"New Schedule"**.
3. Choose active days (e.g. Mon–Fri for work, Every Day for bedtime).
4. Set start time and end time. FocusGuard seamlessly handles overnight schedules (e.g. 22:00 to 07:00).
5. Assign a target profile (e.g. **Sleep Profile**).
6. Toggle **Active**. FocusGuard will automatically engage and disengage the session at the scheduled hours.
